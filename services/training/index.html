Corporate Training: AI Literacy for Leadership

Arm your C-suite and board to lead AI strategy with confidence.

What It Is

Your board understands finance, operations, markets. They do not understand AI. They hear vendor pitches. They read news about AI. They feel exposed. They make decisions without the ground truth.

Corporate training is a customised programme. We teach your executives what AI actually does. We show them where it creates value and where it doesn't. We give them frameworks to evaluate strategy. We build confidence in their decision-making.

What You'll Learn

  • What AI can and cannot do — no hype, no jargon
  • How to evaluate an AI strategy for your business
  • How to govern AI adoption without micromanaging implementation
  • How to measure ROI on AI projects
  • How to build internal capability instead of vendor dependency
  • What other organisations in your sector are actually doing with AI

Format

Option 1: Executive Briefing

Half-day session. Boardroom-ready. Builds strategic literacy in one focused session.

Option 2: Leadership Programme

Three 2-hour sessions over six weeks. Deeper exploration. Hands-on case studies. Board preparation included.

Option 3: Custom Programme

Tailored to your sector, your strategy, your constraints. We interview your team first.

Who Attends

CFOs, COOs, CIOs, board members, senior operations leaders, strategy directors. Anyone making decisions about AI investment.

What Changes

  • Your leadership team speaks the same language about AI.
  • Board decisions are informed, not reactive.
  • You avoid expensive mistakes.
  • You invest with confidence.
