AI Strategy Consulting

Transform organisational capability from strategy to production.

What It Is

You have heard the hype. You know AI matters. But you don't know where to start. You've looked at vendor pitches. You've seen budgets that don't match outcomes. You need clarity.

AI strategy consulting is a structured 90-day engagement. We map your operations. We identify workflows where AI creates measurable business impact. We design a roadmap from pilot to production. We build internal capability so your team owns the system, not a vendor.

How It Works

Phase 1: Discovery

We spend two weeks understanding your business, your workflows, your constraints, your competitive position. We interview decision-makers. We document what matters.

Phase 2: Strategy Design

We design your AI adoption roadmap. We identify 3-5 high-impact pilot workflows. We map resource requirements, timeline, governance. We stress-test the plan against your reality.

Phase 3: Pilot Setup

We prototype one workflow. We train your team. We measure results. We document process so execution can happen without our hand-holding.

Phase 4: Handover & Scale

Your team executes. We review monthly. We remove obstacles. We scale to production.

What Changes

You move from confusion to clarity. From vendor dependency to internal capability. From spending money on AI hype to deploying AI on workflows that matter.

  • You know exactly what AI will and won't do for your business.
  • Your team is trained to own the system.
  • You have measurable outcomes to show the board.

Who This Is For

Financial institutions evaluating AI for risk, compliance, or customer service. Government agencies modernising operations. Education institutions improving administration and student outcomes. Multinational corporations scaling African operations.

Duration: 90 days
