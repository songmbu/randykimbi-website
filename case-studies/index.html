AI strategy for a regional bank's risk operations

The Challenge

Three separate AI initiatives, no shared view of how they connected. The board was unconvinced, risk officers were sceptical, and money was being spent without direction.

The Approach

Two weeks interviewing everyone from the boardroom to the operations floor. The real problem was not technology, it was clarity. We built the strategy framework first, then looked at tools.

The Outcome

A documented roadmap with three prioritised pilots and board approval to scale. The team now owns the system rather than depending on a vendor.

Building AI capability for a government agency

The Challenge

A modernisation goal but no internal expertise, sceptical teams and complex procurement. The traditional path would take 18 months and $2 million.

The Approach

We identified one high-impact workflow, document classification in regulatory review, designed a lightweight solution, trained the team and ran a 90-day pilot in parallel with the existing process.

The Outcome

The agency's own team built and maintains the system. It is in production and delivering daily value, and the team is confident enough to tackle the next workflow.

Training a university leadership team on AI governance

The Challenge

Leadership had no framework for evaluating AI investments. Faculty was sceptical, the IT director wanted to move fast, and the team was divided.

The Approach

A three-session leadership programme tailored to academic priorities: what AI can and cannot do in education, how to evaluate vendors, and governance specific to academic institutions.

The Outcome

A common language across the leadership team, an approved pilot, and a documented AI governance framework with clear decision criteria for future investments.
