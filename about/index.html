Applied AI from the foundation

His degree project was an AI system

Randy’s bachelor’s final project was itself an Artificial Intelligence system — an intelligent EdTech framework that monitors a learner’s attention in real time, detects signs of drowsiness and triggers an audio alert when intervention is needed.

Formal title: “Intelligent EdTech Framework for Real-Time Attention Auditing and Audio-Induced Drowsiness Mitigation.”

In plain terms: an AI system that watches whether a student is still paying attention and wakes them with sound when they drift off — real applied AI, built from the ground up.
