What it is

Clarity before spend.

You have heard the hype and you know AI matters, but you don't know where to start. You have seen vendor pitches and budgets that don't match outcomes. You need clarity first.

AI strategy consulting maps your operations, identifies the workflows where AI creates measurable business impact, and designs a path from pilot to production, building internal capability so your team owns the system.

How it works

A four-phase engagement.

Phase 1

Discovery

Two weeks understanding your business, workflows, constraints and competitive position. We interview decision-makers and document what matters.

Phase 2

Strategy Design

Your adoption roadmap. We identify 3 to 5 high-impact pilot workflows and map resources, timeline and governance, stress-tested against reality.

Phase 3

Pilot Setup

We prototype one workflow, train your team, measure results and document the process so execution can continue without hand-holding.

Phase 4

Handover & Scale

Your team executes. We review monthly, remove obstacles and scale to production.

What changes

Confusion to capability.

  • You know exactly what AI will and will not do for your business.
  • Your team is trained to own the system.
  • You have measurable outcomes to show the board.
