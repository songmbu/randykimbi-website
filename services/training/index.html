What it is

Confidence at the top.

Your board understands finance, operations and markets. They do not yet understand AI. They hear vendor pitches, read the news, and make decisions without the ground truth.

A customised programme teaches your executives what AI actually does, where it creates value and where it does not, and gives them frameworks to evaluate strategy with confidence.

Learning paths

Programmes I teach.

01

Safe AI Use

The core principles of responsible AI, the frameworks to apply them practically inside an enterprise, and the tools for building fairness into AI models.

02

Preparing for the Future of Work with AI Agents

The transformative potential of AI agents at work — from the fundamentals and ethics of agentic AI to practical use in product development and leadership, so your team can implement agents effectively.

03

AI for Managers

Equip managers to lead AI-enabled teams: where AI genuinely helps, how to set realistic expectations, and how to turn tools into measurable output without the hype.

AI for Organisation Leaders

For CEOs, boards and directors: setting AI direction, governing adoption responsibly, and making confident investment decisions that move the whole organisation.
